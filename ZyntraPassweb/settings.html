Privacy and Policy

Here are our core privacy principles:

  • 1. Data Minimization: We only collect the personal data absolutely necessary to provide our event management services.
  • 2. Secure Storage: Your information is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard security measures.
  • 3. No Third-Party Selling: We never sell your personal information or attendee data to third-party marketers or advertisers.
  • 4. User Control: You have the right to access, modify, or permanently delete your account data at any time.
  • 5. Transparent Tracking: We only use essential cookies and tracking for functionality, not for invasive cross-site profiling.
